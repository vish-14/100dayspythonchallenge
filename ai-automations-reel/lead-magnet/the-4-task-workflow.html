The workflow from the reel

The 4-task workflow

Enquiries, follow-ups, documents and team updates: here's how each one runs on its own, with you approving the steps that matter. Use it as a blueprint, or reply to my DM and I'll build it for your business.

The whole flow at a glance
Enquiry→ CRM record→ AI draft→ You approve→ Follow-up timer→ Docs summarised→ Team updated
01

Capture enquiries

Trigger
A new message from your website form, email inbox, Instagram DM or WhatsApp Business.
Steps
  1. Read the message and pull out name, contact details and what they're asking for.
  2. Create or update the contact in your CRM and tag the source.
  3. Ask AI to draft a reply in your tone, using your price list or FAQ.
  4. Send the draft to you for approval; send it only after you approve.
Tools
Make / Zapier / n8nHubSpot · Zoho CRM · Pipedrive · Google SheetsGmail · OutlookChatGPT · Claude
You decide

Every reply is a draft until you tap approve. Nothing reaches a customer without you.

02

Follow up on time

Trigger
A lead hasn't replied, or a quote was sent, after the number of days you choose (for example day 1, 3 and 7).
Steps
  1. Check the CRM every morning for leads that are due.
  2. Remind you, or the owner of the lead, with the context in one message.
  3. Send a follow-up from your pre-approved message templates.
  4. Flag the lead as "needs attention" if there's still no reply after the last step.
Tools
Your CRM's sequencesMake / Zapier / n8nWhatsApp Business · emailSlack · Teams · WhatsApp for reminders
You decide

Only messages you approved in advance go out automatically. Anything unusual comes back to you.

03

Process documents

Trigger
An invoice, purchase order, contract or form arrives by email or lands in a shared folder.
Steps
  1. Read the document and extract the key fields (supplier, number, amount, due date).
  2. Check them against what you expect, such as a matching purchase order.
  3. Write a short summary and save the fields to your sheet or accounting tool.
  4. Queue it for a teammate to check before anything is paid or signed.
Tools
Google Drive · OneDriveChatGPT · Claude (document reading)Google Sheets · Excel · Zoho Books · Xero · QuickBooks
You decide

The AI prepares; a person checks. Mismatches are flagged, never silently fixed.

04

Keep teams updated

Trigger
A task or order changes status: started, done, delayed or blocked.
Steps
  1. Detect the status change in your task tool or CRM.
  2. Write a one-line summary of what changed and what happens next.
  3. Send it only to the people it concerns: sales, operations or finance.
  4. Post a short daily digest so nobody has to ask for an update.
Tools
Trello · Asana · ClickUp · NotionSlack · Microsoft Teams · WhatsApp groups · emailMake / Zapier / n8n
You decide

You set who hears about what. Sensitive updates can always wait for a person.

The three rules that keep it working

Connected tools

Your inbox, CRM, documents and chat share the same data, so nothing is typed twice.

Clear rules

Every automation says exactly when it runs, what it does and when it stops.

Human review

A person approves messages, payments and anything unusual. Every step is logged.

Start this week

  • Write down where your enquiries come in today, and who answers them.
  • Pick one place to keep every contact: a CRM, or even one Google Sheet.
  • Write 3 reply templates you'd be happy to send to any new lead.
  • Decide your follow-up rhythm, for example day 1, day 3 and day 7.
  • Choose the one task that interrupts you most, and automate that first.
