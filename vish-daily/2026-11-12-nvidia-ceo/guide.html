Automation 01

Reply to every enquiry

Every customer gets a fast, useful reply, even when you're busy or away.

Trigger
A new message on WhatsApp Business, Instagram, email or your website form.
Steps
  1. Read the message and pick out what the customer wants.
  2. Reply with your approved answers, prices and next step.
  3. Ask one useful question to qualify the lead.
  4. Save the lead in your CRM and notify the right person.
Tools

Channels: WhatsApp Business, Instagram, email, website form

Records and AI: Google Sheets, Zoho CRM or HubSpot, with ChatGPT or Claude

Automation: Make, Zapier or n8n

Human approval: replies use only the answers and prices you approved. Anything else comes to you.
Automation 02

Follow up automatically

No lead or quote is forgotten because you were busy.

Trigger
A quote is sent, or a customer hasn't replied in the days you set.
Steps
  1. Track every open quote and lead with a next-step date.
  2. Send a polite follow-up from your approved template.
  3. Stop when the customer replies, and alert the owner of the lead.
  4. Mark lost leads with a reason, so you can learn from them.
Tools

Tracking: Your CRM or Google Sheets

Messages: WhatsApp Business or email

Automation: Make, Zapier or n8n

Human approval: you approve the templates and timing once.
Automation 03

Approvals in one tap

The agent does the work, and you approve what matters, from anywhere.

Trigger
The agent drafts a quote, discount, refund or anything unusual.
Steps
  1. Draft the item with all the details you need to decide.
  2. Send it to you on WhatsApp or email with Approve and Edit options.
  3. Send the approved version to the customer.
  4. Log who approved what, and when.
Tools

Approvals: WhatsApp, Slack or email

Documents: Google Docs or your quote template

Logs: Your CRM or a Google Sheet

Human approval: nothing involving money or commitments goes out without you.
Automation 04

Daily summary

You know what happened today without asking anyone.

Trigger
Every evening at the time you choose.
Steps
  1. Collect today's new leads, quotes, payments and open issues.
  2. Write a short summary with what needs your attention.
  3. Send it to you on WhatsApp or email.
  4. Keep a weekly record so you can spot trends.
Tools

Sources: Your CRM, sheets and inbox

AI: ChatGPT or Claude

Delivery: WhatsApp or email

Human approval: read-only: it reports, it doesn't act.
Your first steps

Start this week

Five small steps, no new software needed.

  • List the jobs only you can do today, and how long each takes.
  • Write your top ten replies to customer questions in one document.
  • Move customers and quotes out of your phone into one sheet or CRM.
  • Decide what needs your approval: money, discounts, refunds, exceptions.
  • Pick one workflow and write its steps on one page.
