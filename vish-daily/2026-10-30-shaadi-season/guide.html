Automation 01

Reply to every enquiry

Every family gets a warm, useful reply in minutes, even at night or in the middle of a function.

Trigger
A new message on WhatsApp Business, an Instagram DM or your website form.
Steps
  1. Read the message: family name, date, guest count and what they want.
  2. Check your calendar and reply with open dates and your packages.
  3. Ask the next useful question: venue, events, budget.
  4. Save the enquiry as a new event record and notify you.
Tools

Channels: WhatsApp Business, Instagram, website form

Records and AI: Google Sheets, Zoho CRM or HubSpot, with ChatGPT or Claude

Automation: Make, Zapier or n8n

Human approval: replies use only the packages, prices and dates you approved. Anything else comes to you.
Automation 02

Draft the quote

Families get a clear quote quickly, and you still control every number.

Trigger
The family has shared date, guest count and the services they need.
Steps
  1. Pick the matching package and add the requested services.
  2. Draft the quote in your format with inclusions and terms.
  3. Send it to you for approval, edits or a discount.
  4. Send the approved quote to the family and set a follow-up.
Tools

Documents: Google Docs, Canva or your quote template

AI: ChatGPT or Claude, with your price list

Approval: WhatsApp or email to you

Human approval: no quote reaches a family until you approve it.
Automation 03

Payment reminders

Advances and balances arrive on time, without awkward calls.

Trigger
An advance or balance is due within the days you set.
Steps
  1. Check upcoming due dates every morning.
  2. Send a polite reminder from your approved template, with the payment link.
  3. Mark the payment as received when it arrives.
  4. Tell you about anything still pending after the due date.
Tools

Payments: Razorpay, PhonePe or bank links

Channel: WhatsApp Business or email

Records: your event sheet or CRM

Human approval: only the reminder templates you approved go out, and you handle anything sensitive personally.
Automation 04

Vendor updates

Caterer, decorator, photographer and DJ always have the latest details.

Trigger
A booking is confirmed or a detail changes: date, guest count, menu, timings.
Steps
  1. Detect the change in the event record.
  2. Write a short update for each vendor it affects.
  3. Send it to the vendor or the event's vendor group.
  4. Collect confirmations and flag anyone who has not replied.
Tools

Messages: WhatsApp groups or email

Records: Google Sheets or a CRM

Automation: Make, Zapier or n8n

Human approval: you choose which changes go to which vendors, and big changes wait for you.
Your first steps

Start this week

Five small steps, no new software needed. Each one makes the first build faster.

  • List where enquiries come from: WhatsApp, Instagram, website, referrals.
  • Write your packages in one page, with what each includes and a starting price.
  • Mark your open dates for the season in one calendar.
  • Write three reply templates you would be happy to send to any family.
  • List your regular vendors and how each one prefers to get updates.
