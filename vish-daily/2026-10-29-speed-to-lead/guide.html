Automation 01

Reply instantly

Every new lead gets a helpful reply in minutes, in your tone, even when you're at a viewing or it's late at night.

Trigger
A new enquiry email or notification from a property portal, your website form, Instagram DM or WhatsApp Business.
Steps
  1. Read the enquiry and pull out the name, contact details, listing and question.
  2. Create or update the lead in your CRM, tagged with its portal or channel.
  3. Reply from your approved templates with the listing's facts and viewing days.
  4. Ask one simple question to keep the conversation going, then notify you.
Tools

Automation: Make, Zapier or n8n

CRM: Follow Up Boss, HubSpot, Zoho CRM or a Google Sheet

Channels and AI: Email or WhatsApp Business, with ChatGPT or Claude

Human approval: the AI only sends replies built from templates and listing details you approved. Anything off-script comes to you.
Automation 02

Qualify the lead

You know who is ready to buy or rent before you spend time on a call.

Trigger
The lead replies to the first message.
Steps
  1. Ask about budget, preferred areas, timeline and how they plan to pay.
  2. Save each answer to the lead record in your CRM.
  3. Score the lead as hot, warm or cold using the rules you set.
  4. Summarise the lead in three lines for you or the agent who owns it.
Tools

Conversation: WhatsApp Business, website chat or email

AI: ChatGPT or Claude, with your question list

Records: your CRM's custom fields

Human approval: you set the questions and the scoring rules. The AI never promises prices, availability or terms.
Automation 03

Route and book viewings

Hot leads reach the right agent fast, and viewings get booked without phone tag.

Trigger
A lead is scored hot, or asks to see a property.
Steps
  1. Pick the agent by area, listing or rota, and send them a WhatsApp alert with the summary.
  2. Check that agent's calendar and suggest open viewing slots.
  3. Wait for the agent to approve or change the slot.
  4. Confirm the viewing with the buyer and send a reminder the day before.
Tools

Alerts: WhatsApp, Slack or Microsoft Teams

Calendar: Google Calendar, Outlook or Calendly

Automation: Make, Zapier or n8n

Human approval: no viewing is confirmed until an agent taps approve.
Automation 04

Keep slow leads warm

Buyers who aren't ready yet still think of you when they are.

Trigger
A lead says they're not ready yet, or hasn't replied after the days you set.
Steps
  1. Send a friendly check-in on a rhythm you choose, for example day 2, 7 and 14.
  2. Watch new listings and pick the ones that match the lead's budget and area.
  3. Send the matches with a short note and an easy way to book a viewing.
  4. Move the lead back to hot, and alert you, as soon as they reply with interest.
Tools

Sequences: your CRM's follow-ups, or Make, Zapier or n8n

Listings: your portal feed, website or a Google Sheet

Channels: WhatsApp Business or email

Human approval: only check-ins you approved go out automatically, and leads can opt out at any time.
Your first steps

Start this week

Five small steps, no new software needed. Each one makes the first build faster.

  • List every place leads come from: each portal, your website, Instagram and WhatsApp.
  • Check your own reply time. Send yourself a test enquiry tonight and see when it gets answered.
  • Write three reply templates you'd be happy to send to any new buyer or tenant.
  • Write down your four questions for budget, area, timeline and finance.
  • Decide what makes a lead hot, and who should get the alert.
