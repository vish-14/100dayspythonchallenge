## 8. Lead follow-up (BUILD replies and booked calls)

Three discovery questions for real estate leads:
1. **Which portals and channels send you leads today, and roughly how many a week?** This sizes the instant-reply and CRM work.
2. **When a lead comes in at 9 PM, who replies, and when? What happens if they don't answer your first message?** This shows their real response time and whether follow-up exists.
3. **Where do enquiries and site visits live today: which CRM or sheet, which calendar, WhatsApp personal or Business?** This tells us the integrations and whether WhatsApp Business API onboarding is needed.

Good first pilot: the instant reply plus the qualifier on their single biggest portal, with site visits approved on WhatsApp.
