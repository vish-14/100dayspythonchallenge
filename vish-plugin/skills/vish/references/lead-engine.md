# Lead engine: from reel views to paid AI automation projects

```
Reel (industry pain + workflow)  →  Follow + comment WORKFLOW  →  Auto-DM with the topic PDF
   →  Book a free discovery call (cal.com/vishworkflows/discovery)  or  reply BUILD
   →  Discovery call  →  Scoped proposal  →  Build (pilot first)  →  Case study  →  better reels
```

## 1. Make every reel lead-ready

- **Name the buyer.** Industry reels call out the role ("clinic owners", "real-estate agents") in the hook and the caption's first line, so the right people comment.
- **Sell a mechanism, not hype.** Show trigger → AI → human approval → result. Owners buy when they can picture it in their own business.
- **One keyword forever: WORKFLOW.** Track performance per reel, not per keyword. Each reel gets its own SuperProfile automation pointing to its own PDF.
- **The PDF is the bridge.** It shows exactly what would be built, then asks for the call. Always 2 or 4 workflows, ending with the booking invitation.

## 2. DM conversation (human after the first auto-reply)

When someone replies, answer personally within a few hours. Ask, at most three at a time:
1. "What's your business, and roughly how big is the team?"
2. "Where do [enquiries / orders / documents] come in today, and what tools do you use?"
3. "Which task, if it ran on its own, would save you the most time each week?"

Then: "Easiest is a free discovery call. I'll map the first automation for you: https://cal.com/vishworkflows/discovery".

### Industry discovery questions (adapt per run)

| Industry | Questions that reveal a project |
| --- | --- |
| Clinics | How do patients book today? How many no-shows a week? Which system holds appointments? |
| Real estate | Which portals send leads? How fast do you reply today? Who follows up, and how? |
| E-commerce | Which platform (Shopify, WooCommerce)? What share of tickets are order-status questions? |
| Agencies | How do you onboard clients? How long does monthly reporting take? |
| Accountants | How do clients send documents? Which accounting software? Busiest deadlines? |
| Home services | How many calls are missed? How do you schedule technicians? |
| Any business | What do you copy-paste between tools every day? |

## 3. Discovery call (15 min) → proposal

- Agenda (about 15 min): their process today (5 min) → pick ONE workflow (5 min) → what a pilot looks like (5 min).
- After the call, send within 24 h:
  - a one-page proposal (problem, the workflow, tools, human checkpoints, timeline, price, what they need to provide);
  - a **pilot first**: one workflow, 1–2 weeks. Upsell to more agents once it runs.
- Price on value and scope. Never promise savings or revenue. Promise what will be built and how it will be tested.
- Offer a monthly maintenance and monitoring retainer for every live automation.

## 4. Track it (in `vish-daily/content-log.md`)

Per reel:
- views and reach;
- saves;
- WORKFLOW comments;
- DMs sent;
- BUILD replies;
- calls booked;
- proposals sent;
- projects won.

Each week, look at the rates and feed them back into concept selection:
- comment rate = comments ÷ reach;
- call rate = calls ÷ comments;
- the best industry and pillar.

## 5. Turn wins into content (only with permission)

When a project goes live and the client agrees, it can become:
- a "how I built it" reel;
- a before/after with real, client-approved numbers;
- a "Clients" Highlight.

Until then, use only clearly illustrative examples.

## 6. Don'ts

- Don't mass-DM people who didn't comment.
- Don't buy leads or followers.
- Don't scrape and cold-message from Instagram.
- Don't fake urgency or scarcity.
- Don't share client data in reels.
